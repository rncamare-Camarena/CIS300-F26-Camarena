The Supplies

The main tools and materials, and how each one is used.

What you'll need

Materials

What your piece is made of

  • Glass

    What it is

    Sheets of colored and textured glass made for stained glass projects.

    How it's used

    It's cut into the shapes of your pattern and becomes the colors of your piece.

  • Copper foil

    What it is

    Thin copper tape with a sticky back.

    How it's used

    It wraps the edge of every piece so the solder has something to hold onto.

Tools

What does the work

  • Glass cutter

    What it is

    A handheld tool with a tiny wheel that scores a line on the glass.

    How it's used

    You roll it along your pattern line, then break the glass along the score.

  • Grinder

    What it is

    An electric tool with a diamond-coated bit that wears glass edges down.

    How it's used

    It smooths sharp edges and fine-tunes each piece so everything fits together.

  • Soldering iron

    What it is

    A heated tool that melts solder, a metal that joins the foiled pieces.

    How it's used

    You run melted solder along the foil seams to hold the pieces together.
